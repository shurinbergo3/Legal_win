# Karta Pobytu in Warsaw: When You Can Do It Yourself and When You Need a Lawyer

You can apply for a karta pobytu (residence card) in Warsaw entirely on your own - the state does not require a lawyer, and filing goes through an electronic system. The real question is different: what does a mistake cost you? At the Mazowiecki Urząd Wojewódzki (Mazowieckie Voivodeship Office) a case takes 8 to 14 months to review, and if after a year of waiting an odmowa (refusal) lands because the package was put together wrong, you lose more than the fees. You lose months, and sometimes the legality of your stay. Let us be honest about it: when is a karta pobytu genuinely a do-it-yourself job, and when does saving on a lawyer come back to bite you?

---

## When You Can Genuinely Handle It Yourself

No point pretending otherwise - in simple, "clean" cases you do not need a lawyer. You will most likely manage on your own if:

- you have a **standard basis** - an ordinary employment contract (umowa o pracę, a contract of employment) with a single employer and a stable salary above the minimum;
- there are **no gaps** in your lawful stay, with every visa and status running back to back;
- your **documents are in order**: contract, proof of housing, meldunek (address registration), insurance;
- you are **comfortable** with the Polish language and electronic systems;
- there are **no tricky wrinkles** - no change of employer mid-process, no studies, marriage, children, or business.

For a case like that we have a detailed [step-by-step karta pobytu guide](/en/blog/karta-pobytu-2026-poshagovaya-instrukciya), a walkthrough of [filing through MOS 2.0](/en/blog/kak-podat-zayavlenie-mos-2-0), and a breakdown of [what a karta pobytu costs in Warsaw](/en/blog/skolko-stoit-karta-pobytu-varshava-2026). Read them, put your package together carefully, and there is a fair chance you will not need a lawyer at all.

## When Going Solo Is Risky

Here is where doing it yourself starts turning into a refusal. In our practice in Mazowieckie, these are the situations that most often end in an odmowa for people who filed alone.

### Gaps and a Messy Stay History

If your history has overstays, a change of basis, or long trips abroad, the voivodeship will easily spot a gap you never even suspected when it checks the record of your stay. One unnoticed gap and the whole basis falls apart.

### A Non-Standard Basis

Business (a JDG sole proprietorship or a sp. z o.o. limited company), marriage to a Polish citizen, family reunification, studies, remote work for a foreign employer - each of these carries its own set of requirements and its own typical mistakes. A one-size-fits-all package does not work here.

### Change of Employer or Terms Mid-Process

Changed jobs while the case is being reviewed? Contract terms shifted? That has to be handled correctly and reported to the office, otherwise the basis can start to "slip".

### A Request or a Refusal Has Already Arrived

If the office has sent a request for additional documents (uzupełnienie) with requirements you cannot make sense of, or you have already received an odmowa, this is exactly the moment when the clock runs in days, not weeks.

## What a Mistake Actually Costs

People usually count only the state fee - 340 PLN for the wniosek (application) and 100 PLN for the physical card. But the real cost of a mistake is made of something else entirely:

| What you lose in a refusal | The consequence |
|-------------------------|-------------|
| Time | 8-14 months of waiting wasted, everything from scratch |
| Legal status | Between the refusal and a new filing you can be left with no basis |
| Work | Your right to work can lapse along with your status |
| Nerves and deadlines | The 14 days for an odwołanie (appeal) go by fast |

A refusal is not a "let's just try again, no harm done". It is a reset to a year ago, and sometimes a risk to your entire stay in Poland. Against that, the cost of professional support is usually nowhere near the price of a lost year.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a lawyer to get a karta pobytu in Poland?**
By law, no - you can file yourself through the electronic system. But in non-standard cases (business, marriage, gaps in your stay, a change of employer, a request or refusal already received) the risk of an odmowa without a lawyer is high, and the price of a mistake is a lost year of waiting.

**Can I apply for a karta pobytu in Warsaw myself?**
Yes. With a standard basis (an ordinary employment contract, a clean history, documents in order) many people manage on their own using step-by-step guides. A lawyer is needed where there are contentious wrinkles or where problems have already come up.

**How much does a mistake cost if I file on my own?**
Not just the 340 PLN fee. A refusal costs you 8-14 months of waiting, can leave you with no lawful basis in the meantime, and gives you only 14 days for an odwołanie. That is why, in complex cases, saving on support often turns into far bigger losses.

**What should I do if a refusal (odmowa) has already arrived?**
Act at once - you have 14 days for an odwołanie to the higher authority, then a further 30 days for a skarga to the court. The refusal must be justified in writing, and many grounds can be challenged if you do it properly and on time.

**Will a lawyer help speed up the review?**
You cannot speed it up directly, but when deadlines are broken the law gives you tools - a ponaglenie and a complaint about inaction to the court. We track the status and file them when there are grounds to.
