# JDG for Foreigners in Poland — Opening a Sole Proprietorship in 2025

JDG (jednoosobowa działalność gospodarcza) is the Polish equivalent of a Russian IP, Ukrainian FOP, or Belarusian IP. It is the simplest way to run a business in Poland: registration is free, ZUS contributions are fixed, and tax form is chosen at start. For foreigners, JDG is an excellent path to a **karta pobytu on the basis of działalność gospodarcza**, especially when employment-basis routes do not fit (e.g., freelance with international clients or e-commerce). Below — eligibility, the process, and what it costs in the first year.

## Which Foreigners Can Open a JDG in Poland

Not all foreigners can open a JDG automatically. Conditions:

| Status | JDG eligibility |
|---|---|
| Karta stałego pobytu | yes, no restrictions |
| Karta rezydenta długoterminowego UE | yes |
| Karta CUKR (UA with PESEL UKR) | yes |
| Karta Polaka | yes |
| Karta Błękitna UE | yes |
| Karta czasowego pobytu (most bases) | **no** for most, but **yes** for "działalność gospodarcza" basis |
| D visa | no (need karta pobytu by business first) |
| Visa-free regime | no |

So: with a karta czasowego pobytu **by employment**, you cannot open a JDG without first obtaining a separate karta pobytu **by business**. This is a common misunderstanding.

Exception — if you have **karta CUKR**: it grants the right to JDG automatically without a separate karta.

## Advantages of JDG for a Foreigner

1. **Client flexibility** — work with Polish, foreign, and individual clients simultaneously
2. **Lower tax in the first year** — ulga na start (full ZUS exemption for 6 months) + small ZUS for the next 24 months
3. **Eligibility for karta czasowego pobytu by działalność** — no fixed-salary requirement
4. **Simplified accounting** — for small turnover you can self-manage (PIT-28 or PIT-36)
5. **VAT deductions** — once registered as vatowiec
6. **Counted toward stałego pobytu** — JDG years count as ordinary work

## Tax Forms — Choice at Start

When opening JDG, choose one of three taxation forms:

| Form | Rate 2025 | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| **Zasady ogólne (skala podatkowa)** | 12% up to 120,000 + 32% above | businesses with high deductibles |
| **Podatek liniowy** | 19% on income | IT, consulting (no child deductions) |
| **Ryczałt** | 3–17% on revenue (not income) | services, retail, simple business |

For most IT freelancers and consultants, **ryczałt 12%** or **podatek liniowy 19%** are the optimal options.

## ZUS — The Most Confusing Payments

ZUS is mandatory insurance (pension, health, occupational). Structure:

| JDG period | Monthly ZUS in 2025 |
|---|---|
| First 6 months (ulga na start) | ~167 PLN (zdrowotne only) |
| Next 24 months (mały ZUS) | ~770 PLN (preferential) |
| After 30 months | ~1,600 PLN (standard) |

This is a critical financial parameter — JDG without revenue above ~5,000 PLN/month becomes uneconomic in year 4.

In 2025 there is also "mały ZUS plus" — for those whose previous-year income did not exceed 120,000 PLN. ZUS is income-proportional, typically 800–1,200 PLN/month.

## JDG Registration — Step by Step

**Step 1.** Obtain PESEL (if not yet) and Profil Zaufany.

**Step 2.** Determine PKD codes — codes for business activities from the Polish Activity Classification. For a typical IT freelancer: 62.01.Z (oprogramowanie), 70.22.Z (doradztwo), 73.11.Z (reklamy). Up to 9 codes can be listed, with one as główny.

**Step 3.** Complete CEIDG-1. Form on ceidg.gov.pl, online via Profil Zaufany. Fields: PESEL, name, address, PKD, taxation form, ZUS code, bank account.

**Step 4.** File via ePUAP or sign in person at Urząd Gminy. Registration is free, takes 1–7 days.

**Step 5.** Receive NIP (automatically), REGON (automatically), and ZUS number. All documents appear in your personal account at ceidg.gov.pl.

**Step 6.** If revenue will exceed 200,000 PLN/year — register as VAT-payer (vatowiec). Below 200,000 PLN — remain zwolniony from VAT (zwolnienie podmiotowe).

**Step 7.** Open a business bank account (Santander, mBank Firmy, PKO Firmowe). 30–50 PLN/month.

## Reporting and Taxes — What to Do

| Payment | Deadline | To whom |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly ZUS | by 20th of following month | ZUS portal |
| Zaliczka on PIT (under ryczałt or liniowy) | by 20th of following month | US (Urząd Skarbowy) |
| VAT (if vatowiec) | by 25th of following month | US |
| Annual PIT-28 (ryczałt) | by 28 February of following year | US |
| Annual PIT-36 (zasady ogólne) | by 30 April of following year | US |

An accountant for JDG is optional (you can use apps like iFirma, inFakt, wFirma — 30–100 PLN/month). But if revenue exceeds 5,000 PLN/month, hiring an accountant for 200–400 PLN/month is standard.

## Karta Pobytu by Działalność Gospodarcza

If you have karta czasowego pobytu **by employment** and want to switch to JDG, you need a new karta **by business**. Conditions for this basis (Ustawa o cudzoziemcach, art. 142):

- JDG income at least **12× national average annually** (approximately 90,000–100,000 PLN/year in 2025)
- OR at least **2 employees** who are PR holders or Polish citizens
- Genuine business (not an "empty" JDG)
- ZUS contributions current
- Full CEIDG history available for review

For a small IT freelance with 50,000 PLN income, this criterion **is not met**, and karta pobytu by działalność will not be issued. Apply on employment with a major client or find ways to reach the 12× base.

## Most Common Mistakes

**1. Opening JDG without karta pobytu by działalność.** If the main karta is by employment, JDG does not officially license you as a sole trader. Tax authorities accept declarations, but the voivode may treat it as a violation.

**2. Wrong PKD.** PKD that does not match real activity — issues at VAT inspections or karta pobytu reviews.

**3. Missed ZUS payments.** Each missed month — fine + penalties. After 3–6 months — debt-collection committee.

**4. Late PIT filing.** PIT delays — 1–7% annual fines on tax + interest.

**5. Forgetting ZUS registration.** After CEIDG registration, you must separately submit ZUS ZUA (insurance registration). Many forget and incur fines.

## When You Need a Lawyer or Accountant

Self-registration of JDG is realistic if you already have rights (karta CUKR, karta stałego, Polish citizenship) and are ready to spend 5–10 hours studying CEIDG, ZUS, US.

A lawyer makes sense if:

- Opening JDG in parallel with karta pobytu by działalność
- Complex structure (multiple income sources, foreign clients)
- VAT registration with EU/non-EU clients
- Prior refusal or JDG closure
- Planning to hire employees via ZUS
