# Tax and social security clearance certificates in Poland: where and how

Two documents get requested in Poland more often than anything except a company register extract: the **ZUS certificate of no arrears in social security contributions** and the **tax office certificate of no tax arrears**. The first is free; the second costs PLN 21. Both take a day or two, provided there are no arrears.

The difficulty is rarely in obtaining them. It is in the timing: someone asks for them at short notice, and the system surfaces a twelve-zloty underpayment from three years ago.

## Where they are required

| Situation | ZUS | Tax office |
|---|---|---|
| Residence card on business grounds | yes | yes |
| Permanent residence and EU long-term resident status | yes | yes |
| Tenders and public procurement | yes | yes |
| Loans, leasing, factoring | often | often |
| Carrier licence | yes | yes |
| Selling a company, due diligence | yes | yes |
| Changing tax form, applying for grants | sometimes | yes |
| Apostille for a foreign counterparty | on request | on request |

For a foreign entrepreneur the first two rows matter most. In a business-based residence case the wojewoda examines not only income but payment discipline: arrears at ZUS read as instability in the business. We covered the income thresholds [in the business grounds article](/en/blog/karta-pobytu-udalennaya-rabota-2026).

## How to obtain them

**ZUS.** Through the PUE ZUS portal - the application is filed online and the certificate usually arrives electronically within a day, sometimes immediately. Paper versions come from a branch. **Cost: PLN 0.**

**Tax office.** Through the e-tax office or on paper, using form ZAS-W. **Stamp duty: PLN 21.** One day to a week, depending on the office.

**Through a representative.** Both are obtainable under a power of attorney - ZUS-PEL for social security, PPS-1 or PPO-1 for tax. That is how we work: no logins, no queues.

One point to note: **electronic certificates work inside Poland**, but an apostille needs a paper original. If the document is going abroad, order paper from the start. [Why that is](/en/blog/e-apostille-elektronnye-dokumenty-2026).

## How fresh they must be

There is no statutory expiry; the certificate reflects the position on the day of issue. In practice:

- **the wojewoda** - usually accepts one no older than one to three months;
- **tenders** - typically three months, sometimes six;
- **banks** - most often 30 days;
- **leasing and factoring** - 30 to 90 days.

Ordering in advance is pointless: the position can change in a week and a fresh certificate costs 21 zloty.

## When arrears do exist

The familiar scenario: the certificate is needed tomorrow and the system shows a debt. Options, fastest first:

**1. Pay and wait for posting.** Payments post at ZUS in one to three working days and at the tax office in one to five. The quickest route for small amounts.

**2. Check whether the debt is real.** Half the "arrears" we see are posting errors: a payment sent to the wrong contribution account, a return filed with a typo in the tax ID, or a contribution allocated to the wrong period. The review takes a day and costs nothing to fix.

**3. An instalment arrangement or deferral.** For larger sums both ZUS and the tax office allow instalments. Once agreed, a **certificate of no arrears taking the instalment arrangement into account** is issued, and many procedures accept it on a par with a clean certificate. Not all of them, so confirm the requirement first.

**4. Check limitation periods.** Some older liabilities may be time-barred; that is assessed case by case.

## A detail about limited companies

A company needs certificates for **the entity itself**. In tenders and for some licences, documents concerning board members are added - and those cover their personal liabilities, not the company's.

Tenders also call for the collective entity criminal record certificate - [a separate document](/en/blog/krk-o-podmiocie-zbiorowym-dlya-firmy-2026) frequently confused with clearance certificates.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the ZUS certificate chargeable?**
No. Only the tax certificate carries a fee, of PLN 21.

**My company was registered last month. Will they issue one?**
Yes; the system will show no arrears. The company must be registered and hold a tax ID.

**Are the certificates needed if my sole trader business is suspended?**
Yes. Suspension does not cancel liabilities from earlier periods.

**Will the wojewoda accept an electronic certificate?**
Yes - a file or scan is attached when filing through MOS v2.0. Paper is only needed for an apostille.

**The arrears are five zloty. Does that really block the certificate?**
Formally, yes - any arrears prevent a clean certificate. Which is why checking the balance comes first.

**Can I get a certificate for a past period?**
The certificate reflects the position on the day of issue. History is proven with other documents, such as ZUS insurance period certificates.
