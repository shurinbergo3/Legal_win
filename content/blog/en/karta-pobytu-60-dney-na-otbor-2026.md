# The 60-Day Rule: How Not to Lose Your Ready Karta Pobytu in 2026

You have cleared the hard part - filed the wniosek (application), given your biometrics, waited out a positive decision. Looks like the finish line. But it is precisely at this last step that 2026 planted a trap few people warn you about: fail to collect your ready karta pobytu (residence card) in time and the permit is annulled, sending the whole saga back to the start. This is the new 60-day rule.

It sounds like a formality, but real losses sit behind it - months of waiting, fees you already paid, and a fresh spot back in the queue. Below is what the rule means, how to learn in time that your card is ready, and what to do if you did miss the deadline.

---

## What the 60-Day Rule Means

From 2026 there is a rule: if you do not collect your ready karta pobytu within 60 days of the urząd's notice that it is ready, the zezwolenie (permit) is not issued and the card is annulled. In effect, the positive decision on your application loses its force, and to obtain the status you have to file a wniosek all over again.

Let us look at why this is so serious and how the rule plays out in practice.

### Why It Matters More Than It Seems

People used to treat collecting the plastic casually: the decision is in, the card is being made somewhere, I will pick it up when I have time. In 2026 that logic gets expensive. What is annulled is not merely a plastic card - the residence permit itself, the one you waited months for, loses its force.

The consequences of missing the deadline:

- the opłata skarbowa (stamp duty) of 340 PLN and the 100 PLN for the plastic are not refunded;
- the 8-14 months you spent waiting are gone for nothing;
- you have to file a wniosek again and wait all over;
- in the gap it is easy to end up without a valid basis for staying.

---

## How to Know Your Card Is Ready

The 60 days start running not when the card is physically produced, but from the urząd's notice. So it is critical not to miss the notice itself. There are several channels, and you should not rely on just one.

### The Official Notice From the Urząd

The wojewoda (regional governor) informs you that the card is ready and that you need to come in for it. The notice can arrive by different routes - through the system, via electronic doręczenia (official deliveries), or another way the procedure allows. The problem is that notices get lost: an address changed, the right channel went unchecked, the letter landed in spam. And the clock keeps ticking.

### Checking the Status Yourself

Do not wait passively. You can and should track the case yourself:

| Method | What it gives you |
|--------|-------------------|
| Your account in the filing system | Current status of the wniosek and decision |
| The wojewoda's online card-readiness check | Information on whether the plastic is ready |
| Direct contact with the urząd | A specific update on your case |
| Support from a representative | Status monitoring done for you |

We advise checking the status at least once a week after a positive decision comes through. That is exactly the window in which cards "burn up" for people who relaxed at the finish.

---

## Collecting the Card by Power of Attorney

A frequent situation: the card is ready, but you are on a work trip, away, ill, or otherwise unable to reach the urząd within the 60 days. There is a way out - collection through a representative under a pełnomocnictwo (power of attorney).

Points to keep in mind:

- the pełnomocnictwo is drawn up for a specific person and a specific act - collecting the karta pobytu;
- the representative comes with the power of attorney and their own ID;
- in some cases your personal presence or extra steps are still needed to activate the card, so this is worth confirming in advance for your specific case.

For that reason, if you know you will be out of Poland over the coming two months, sort out the power of attorney not on the last day but right after the decision arrives.

---

## What to Do If You Missed the Deadline for a Valid Reason

There is more than one way to miss the 60 days. Forgetting is one thing; being in hospital, stuck abroad through no fault of your own, or never getting the notice because of a delivery error is another.

If the deadline was missed for a valid reason, do not write everything off just yet. Administrative procedure has a mechanism for reinstating a missed deadline, przywrócenie terminu. The key conditions:

- the reason must be genuinely valid and, ideally, documented (a hospital certificate, records of being abroad);
- you must apply quickly, as soon as the obstacle is gone;
- the decision rests with the urząd - the deadline is not restored automatically.

Here your argument and the timeliness of your request are what count. If the card was annulled and you believe the reason for missing the deadline was valid, that is grounds to fight, not to give up. In disputed cases involving refusals and reinstated deadlines, we help through [appeal support](/en/uslugi/apelacje).

## How to Avoid the Trap: A Checklist

To keep the 60-day rule from catching you off guard, stay on top of a few things:

1. **Track the status after a positive decision.** Do not wait passively for a letter - check for yourself.
2. **Keep your contact details and address current** in the system so the notice reaches you.
3. **Check every notification channel**, including electronic doręczenia.
4. **Plan trips with the card's likely readiness in mind.** If you are waiting on a decision, a long trip is better timed around it.
5. **Prepare a power of attorney in advance** if you sense you might not make it in person.
6. **If you miss the deadline, act at once** - the sooner you apply to reinstate it, the better your odds.

One related rule worth repeating: if the card is lost or stolen, you have 14 days to notify the wojewoda and file for a duplicate - we covered that in our article on [losing your card and the wtórnik](/en/blog/karta-pobytu-utrata-kradzha-wtornik-2026). Deadlines in immigration cases are short, and this is one of those times when the calendar works against you.
