# How to Become a Professional Driver in Poland 2026: Licence, Kod 95, Karta Kierowcy

Most drivers from Ukraine, Belarus and other CIS countries assume that to work long-haul or on a bus in Poland it is enough to exchange the licence. In reality there are three documents, and each covers its own task: a **prawo jazdy** (driving licence) of the right category, **Kod 95** (certificate of professional competence) and a **karta kierowcy** (tachograph driver card). Without any one of them an employer cannot send you on a run, and ITD will fine you on the road. This article is an overview of the whole route: what you need, in what order, and how much it costs. For the detail of each step we link to separate breakdowns.

## In short: what to know in 30 seconds

- **Three documents you cannot work as a truck or bus driver without:** a Polish licence of category C/CE or D/DE, Kod 95 and karta kierowcy. These are three DIFFERENT things, not one and the same.
- **Sequence for a CIS foreigner:** legal stay (karta pobytu / PESEL) → exchange the foreign licence for a Polish one → Kod 95 → karta kierowcy → work.
- **Total cost** of licence exchange, Kod 95 and karta kierowcy for category C/D is roughly 1,900 to 4,000 PLN, excluding legal support fees.
- **Timeline from scratch:** 2 to 5 months, mainly because of the wait for the licence exchange and the state exam for Kwalifikacja Wstępna.
- **For category B** (cars, courier delivery up to 3.5 tonnes) Kod 95 and karta kierowcy are not required - only the licence exchange.

## The three documents you cannot work as a driver without

These three documents are often confused with one another. The licence lets you drive the vehicle, Kod 95 gives you the right to do it for money as a profession, and the karta kierowcy records your working time in the tachograph. One does not replace another.

| Document | What it confirms | Who issues it | Cost | Validity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prawo jazdy cat. C/CE, D/DE | Right to drive a truck/bus | Wydział Komunikacji | fee 100.50 PLN; cat. C/D exchange ~600-900 PLN | 5 years (for C/D with medical) |
| Kod 95 | Right to work as a professional driver in the EU | Wydział Komunikacji (after course) | ~800-3,200 PLN depending on the path | 5 years |
| Karta kierowcy | Recording of working time in the tachograph | PWPW via info-car.pl | 172.20 PLN | 5 years |

Now each step in order.

## Step 1. Legal stay and PESEL

Before arranging anything, you need legal stay in Poland and a Polish **PESEL** (identification number). Without them you will not be issued a **PKZ** (Profil Kierowcy Zawodowego - professional driver profile), without which you cannot enrol on the Kod 95 course, and your licence-exchange application will not be accepted.

The basic document is a **karta pobytu** (residence card) or another proof of legalny pobyt (legal stay): a D visa, or PESEL UKR with a meldunek (address registration). Ukrainians under temporary protection use PESEL UKR; others need a karta pobytu or the relevant visa.

If you have only just arrived and legalisation is not yet complete, start with it. Details and support are in our [karta pobytu and legalisation](/en/uslugi/karta-pobytu) service. Without this step the other documents cannot be arranged.

## Step 2. Exchanging the licence for a Polish one

Under Polish law (Ustawa o kierujących pojazdami, art. 14), after **185 days** of residence in Poland a foreign licence loses its legal validity for driving. It must be exchanged for a Polish one - for categories A and B (and D for Vienna Convention countries) without a theory or practical exam.

Key figures:

- Fee - **100.50 PLN** (100 PLN opłata komunikacyjna + 0.50 PLN ewidencyjna).
- Exchange of categories C/D with a medical and a psychological assessment - roughly **600-900 PLN** in total.
- Timelines: for a Ukrainian licence - **30-60 days**, for a Belarusian one - **60-90 days** (depending on the reply from the issuing country's interior ministry).
- Ukraine and Belarus are part of the Vienna Convention, so the exchange is done without exams.

We covered the process country by country in three pieces: the [general exchange guide](/en/blog/obmen-prav-na-polskie-2026), [exchanging a Ukrainian licence](/en/blog/obmen-ukrainskih-prav-na-polskie-2026) and [exchanging a Belarusian licence](/en/blog/obmen-belorusskih-prav-na-polskie-2026). Support is in the [wymiana prawa jazdy](/en/uslugi/wymiana-prawa-jazdy) service.

An important nuance for Ukrainians: with PESEL UKR your Ukrainian licence remains valid for **personal** driving until the end of temporary protection (the current term is March 2027). But to **work** as a driver, a Polish licence plus Kod 95 and karta kierowcy are still required. More on the 185-day trap is in our [185-day rule and ITD fines](/en/blog/pravilo-185-dney-itd-shtrafy) breakdown.

## Step 3. Kod 95 - which path is yours

Kod 95 is the entry "95" on the licence (or a separate karta kwalifikacji) confirming you hold professional competence under Directive 2003/59/EC. The legal basis in Poland is Ustawa o transporcie drogowym, art. 39a. It is required only for categories **C/CE and D/DE**; for category B - not.

The path depends **solely on the date your category C was first issued** (for D - similarly):

- **Category C issued before 10 September 2009** → a **short** Szkolenie Okresowe course is enough: 35 hours, no exam, ~800-1,200 PLN.
- **Category C issued on 10 September 2009 or later** → you need the **long** Kwalifikacja Wstępna path: 140 hours plus a state exam at WORD, ~2,500-3,200 PLN.

Kod 95 is valid for **5 years**; renewal is via Szkolenie Okresowe (35 hours). Before the course you must set up a PKZ at the Wydział Komunikacji - this requires a Polish PESEL and legal stay. The karta kwalifikacji plastic card is 100.50 PLN.

How to pin down your path exactly and complete each one is in two breakdowns: [Kod 95: step-by-step guide](/en/blog/kod-95-polsha-kak-poluchit) and [long course vs short](/en/blog/kod-95-kwalifikacja-wstepna-vs-szkolenie-okresowe). Support is in the [Kod 95](/en/uslugi/kod-95) service.

## Step 4. Karta kierowcy for the tachograph

The **karta kierowcy** (driver card) is a personal smart card you insert into the tachograph. It records your time at the wheel, your rest and your breaks. Without it you have no right to start a run in a vehicle with a tachograph, even if the licence and Kod 95 are in order.

Key figures:

- Issued by **PWPW** (the Polish state security printing works) via the **info-car.pl** portal.
- Cost - **172.20 PLN**, validity - **5 years**.
- Online processing time - about **7 days**.
- Since 19 May 2023 a foreigner may apply with a **foreign** licence (plus proof of its authenticity), without waiting for the exchange to finish.

The application process is covered in detail in [karta kierowcy for the tachograph](/en/blog/karta-kierowcy-tachograf-polsha-2026).

## How much the whole route costs and how long it takes

Below is a summary of the official fees and courses. Legal support fees are not included here.

| Stage | Cost | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Licence exchange cat. C/D | ~600-900 PLN (fee 100.50 PLN included) | UA 30-60 days, BY 60-90 days |
| Kod 95 - Szkolenie Okresowe (short) | ~800-1,200 PLN + plastic 100.50 PLN | 2-3 weeks |
| Kod 95 - Kwalifikacja Wstępna (long) | ~2,500-3,200 PLN + plastic 100.50 PLN | 7-9 weeks with exam |
| Karta kierowcy | 172.20 PLN | ~7 days |
| **Total (short Kod 95 path)** | **~1,900-2,600 PLN** | **~1.5-2 months** |
| **Total (long Kod 95 path)** | **~3,400-4,400 PLN** | **~3-5 months** |

The longest part is the licence exchange (waiting for the reply from the issuing country's interior ministry) and the state exam for Kwalifikacja Wstępna. If you arrange things in parallel - for example, apply for the karta kierowcy on the foreign licence while the exchange runs - the total time shortens.

## Common mistakes

**Thinking Kod 95 = karta kierowcy.** These are different documents. Kod 95 gives you the right to work as a driver as a profession; the karta kierowcy records working time in the tachograph. You need both.

**Forgetting to exchange the licence.** People obtain Kod 95 or a karta kierowcy on a foreign licence and assume they are ready. But after 185 days of residence the foreign licence is invalid for driving, and a PKZ for Kod 95 will not be set up without a Polish category C/D licence.

**Assuming PESEL UKR = the right to work as a driver.** PESEL UKR and a valid Ukrainian licence let you drive privately, but not work as a professional driver. To work you need a Polish licence, Kod 95 and a karta kierowcy.

**Choosing the wrong Kod 95 path.** Taking the short course even though category C was issued after 10 September 2009. ITD will check the date on the road and declare the Kod 95 invalid.

**Ignoring ITD checks.** Since August 2025 ITD has been running mass roadside Kod 95 checks. Fines run up to **8,000-12,000 PLN**, mostly on the employer, but risking a run is not worth it. More is in our [ITD and BAG fines up to 12,000 PLN](/en/blog/shtrafy-itd-bag-12000-pln-vodiel) breakdown.

## When to involve a lawyer

Going through the route yourself is realistic, especially if your Polish is at B1+ and you have time to read Polish instructions. A lawyer or support service makes sense if:

- You are arranging karta pobytu, the licence exchange and Kod 95 at once - as a package it is faster and often cheaper than three separate processes.
- There is a category mismatch between countries or an unclear first issue date for category C.
- A haulage employer wants Kod 95 "yesterday" and the timeline has to be compressed.
- You need remote handling via pełnomocnictwo (power of attorney) - for example, you are currently working in another EU country.
