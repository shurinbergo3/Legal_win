# Enrolling Children in a Polish School — 2024 Guide for Foreigners

Children of foreigners in Poland are entitled to **free education** in the public system on the same terms as Polish children — enshrined in the Polish Constitution (art. 70) and the Ustawa o systemie oświaty. The enrolment procedure differs from the one for Polish citizens: translations of documents are required, sometimes apostilles, and an understanding of the system of szkoły podstawowe, liceá, technika, and szkoły branżowe. Below — what to do in Warsaw in 2024 to secure your child a school place.

## Ages and Types of Schools

The Polish education system:

| Age | School type | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| 3–6 years | przedszkole (kindergarten) | — |
| 6–7 years | zerówka (preparatory) | 0 |
| 7–15 years | szkoła podstawowa (primary + lower secondary) | 1–8 |
| 15–19 years | liceum ogólnokształcące (general high school) | 1–4 |
| 15–19/20 years | technikum (technical + general) | 1–5 |
| 15–18 years | szkoła branżowa (vocational) | 1–3 |

Children aged 7–15 have a school obligation (obowiązek szkolny). After podstawówka — minimum 3 years of any secondary school is mandatory (obowiązek nauki to age 18).

## Enrolling in Szkoła Podstawowa — Step by Step

**Step 1.** Determine the rejon of your residence. In Warsaw every school has a "mandatory rejon" — streets whose meldunek-registered children have a guaranteed place. Check at warszawa19115.pl or edukacja.warszawa.pl.

**Step 2.** Gather documents:

- Birth certificate of the child with apostille + sworn translation
- Child's passport (or parent's passport if child is registered there)
- Vaccination certificate (health record) with translation
- School transcript — academic "świadectwo" with grades for the past 2–3 years + translation
- Karta pobytu or karta CUKR of the child (for UA — PESEL UKR is sufficient)
- Meldunek at residence address (or proof of residence via lease)

**Step 3.** Submit the wniosek to the chosen school. In Warsaw enrolment is via **rekrutacja-warszawa.pl** (main periods: March–April). For mid-year enrolment (arrived during the year) — directly to the principal.

**Step 4.** The school accepts or directs to a neighbouring school with capacity. Polish law requires the Warsaw authorities to provide a place for every child with legalny pobyt.

## Language Classes — Critical Element

Foreign children receive **additional Polish lessons** — up to 6 hours per week, free of charge, for up to 24 months of study. These are supplementary classes typically scheduled alongside regular lessons.

To obtain these hours, parents file a "wniosek o organizację dodatkowych zajęć języka polskiego" in the first weeks after enrolment. Most schools have status teachers — "asystent kulturowy" (cultural assistants) — for classes with many foreign students (often Ukrainians).

## Free School Meals

In Poland school lunches (obiad) are paid — usually 8–15 PLN per meal. However, for low-income families (below 1,052 PLN per family member in 2024) — **free meals via MOPS** (Miejski Ośrodek Pomocy Społecznej). Application via warszawa19115.pl, "Pomoc społeczna" section.

## Liceum, Technikum, Szkoła Branżowa

After grade 8 of szkoła podstawowa, the child chooses a secondary school. Enrolment based on results of the egzamin ósmoklasisty (mandatory for all in Poland) and grade-8 marks.

For foreign children arriving in Poland during higher grades:

- **If there is an analogous programme** in the country of origin (secondary school certificate or equivalent), **uznanie świadectwa zagranicznego** is processed via the kuratorium oświaty (1–3 months)
- **If the child has not yet completed secondary education** in the country of origin, they are placed in the corresponding grade with additional Polish hours

Enrolment via rekrutacja-warszawa.pl from May to July for the following academic year.

## Special Cases

**Ukrainian children with PESEL UKR.** Special integration programme: guaranteed place + additional Polish hours + free meals (if the family receives UKR-related benefits). Enrolment via a dedicated form on warszawa19115.pl.

**Children without Polish.** "Oddziały przygotowawcze" — preparatory classes for foreign children with intensive Polish (up to 12 hours/week) for 1–2 years. After them — transfer to a regular class.

**Weekend / online schools.** For children whose parents plan to return to the country of origin in 1–2 years, there are weekend-school options (e.g., the Ukrainian embassy in Warsaw organises Saturday Ukrainian-curriculum classes) or remote learning via Ukrainian/Belarusian online schools. This does not replace obowiązek szkolny but can serve as a supplement.

## Costs — Itemised

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Public podstawówka, liceum, technikum | free |
| Textbooks | free via subsidies |
| Document sworn translations | 200–500 PLN one-off |
| Apostille on birth certificates | 50–100 PLN one-off |
| School lunches | 160–300 PLN/month (or free via MOPS) |
| School supplies | 100–200 PLN at start of year |
| School activities (trips, theatre) | 30–100 PLN/year |

Private international schools in Warsaw (American School, British School, French School, Lycée Français Varsovie) — 30,000–80,000 PLN/year, instruction in English/French.

## Most Common Mistakes

1. **Late sworn translations.** The birth certificate must be translated **before** enrolment, not after. Miss this — the place is lost.
2. **Applying to only one school.** In rekrutacja-warszawa.pl you can apply to 5–7 schools by priority. Always apply to several.
3. **Skipping additional Polish lessons.** Many parents forget to submit the wniosek in the first 2 weeks — getting hours later is harder.
4. **No meldunek.** Schools may decline if the child has no meldunek, citing lack of guaranteed place in the rejon.
5. **Missing transcript from previous school.** Without academic history, a school may place the child a grade below their level.
