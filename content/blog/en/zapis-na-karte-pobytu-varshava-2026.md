# Karta Pobytu Appointment in Warsaw 2026: Termin and MOS v2.0

Every week people come to us with the same question: how do I catch an appointment to submit my karta pobytu documents in Warsaw. In their heads sits the old picture - inPOL, where you sat at the screen at night hunting for a free slot. That picture is out of date. Since December 2025 the filing moved online, and the "appointment to submit" as a thing is gone. What remains is a single real in-person date - the termin (visit date) for biometrics.

In short: since 1 December 2025, the karta pobytu application is filed online through MOS v2.0, and there is no queue to hunt for - you send the wniosek (application) from home. There is now only one in-person termin, for biometrics, and the Mazowiecki Urząd assigns it itself, usually 30-60 days after the UPO (electronic confirmation of receipt). A decyzja (decision) in Mazovia in 2026 takes 8-14 months.

What follows is the practical, step-by-step version.

## What changed: "booking" is no longer a queue

Until December 2025, submitting karta pobytu documents in Mazovia really was a race against the clock. The inPOL system released a limited number of in-person slots, they vanished within minutes, and people spent months "hunting for an appointment" just to hand a folder of documents over a counter. Hence the search people still arrive with: "karta pobytu appointment Warsaw".

Since 1 December 2025, inPOL has been shut down. All residence applications - karta czasowego pobytu, karta stałego pobytu, karta CUKR, extensions, change-of-employer notifications - are filed exclusively through MOS v2.0 (mos.cudzoziemcy.gov.pl). Filing is now fully electronic. That means:

- To file the wniosek, you need no in-person visit at all. You fill it in and send it online.
- There are no "submission slots" left to hunt for. You can file any day, even at three in the morning.
- The old logic of "book first, then come and hand in documents" no longer applies. You file first, and only then does the office assign the single in-person visit - for biometrics.

So the word "booking" stayed in people's heads, but the reality underneath it changed. There is no kolejka (queue) for filing in MOS v2.0. There is only a termin for fingerprints, and the office, not the applicant, sets it.

Logging in to MOS v2.0 is via Profil Zaufany, login.gov.pl, e-dowód or mDowód. Without one of these identifiers you cannot enter the system, so the first thing to do is to activate Profil Zaufany (fastest through the online banking of a major bank).

## How to file through MOS v2.0 step by step

Technically, filing takes one day. The logic is short:

1. You create an account on mos.cudzoziemcy.gov.pl and confirm your identity through Profil Zaufany or login.gov.pl.
2. You start a new sprawa (case), choose the type ("pobyt czasowy", "pobyt stały", etc.) and the basis - praca, działalność, nauka, rodzina, and others.
3. You complete the wniosek and upload document scans (PDF up to 10 MB per file).
4. You send your employer a request to e-sign Załącznik nr 1 - they sign it through their own Profil Zaufany.
5. You sign the wniosek with your Profil Zaufany and receive a UPO (Urzędowe Poświadczenie Odbioru) with the case number.

From the moment of the UPO your stay in Poland is legal, even if your visa or stamp expires tomorrow. The UPO must be saved and printed - you show it at the border and to your employer.

After that, correspondence with the wojewoda goes solely through eDoręczenia (the electronic system of official notifications), not on paper and not by email. The deadline to answer a request from the wojewoda is usually 7 days from delivery of the message. Miss it, and the case can be left bez rozpoznania (without examination), and you have to file again.

A detailed walkthrough of every MOS screen is in the guide on [how to file through MOS 2.0](/en/blog/kak-podat-zayavlenie-mos-2-0). The full picture of the process, with documents and timelines, is in the [step-by-step karta pobytu 2026 instruction](/en/blog/karta-pobytu-2026-poshagovaya-instrukciya).

The most common breakdown at this step is an employer without Profil Zaufany and eDoręczenia, who cannot sign Załącznik nr 1. Solve it in advance: ask your employer to activate both services before you start filing, otherwise the process stalls for 2-3 weeks.

## The biometrics termin in Mazovia: when and where

This is the only "booking" that remains real. Once the wojewoda admits the case for examination, you receive a summons to give fingerprints and a signature on a reader tablet - this is the biometrics.

A few important details:

- **The office sets the date, not you.** The Mazowiecki Urząd Wojewódzki picks the termin itself and sends it to eDoręczenia. There is no slot-hunting like in inPOL.
- **Timing.** The summons usually arrives 30-60 days after the UPO.
- **Where it is taken.** In Mazovia, biometrics are collected at al. Solidarności 81 or at the filia (branch) at ul. Krucza 5/11 - the exact address depends on the district your meldunek belongs to.

What to bring: the original passport, the printed UPO, and any second photo ID (an old karta pobytu, a driving licence). The procedure itself takes about 15 minutes - prints and a signature, with no interrogation and no language tests.

## What to do if the termin takes too long

Sometimes the biometrics summons is delayed beyond the usual 30-60 days. Before you worry, check the mechanics:

- **Check eDoręczenia once a week.** The summons lands there. An SMS alerts you to the first message from the wojewoda; for later ones, it does not. It happens that a termin is already assigned but the person does not see it, because they never opened eDoręczenia.
- **Check whether there was a request to uzupełnienie (supply missing documents).** If the wojewoda is waiting on a document, the case "hangs" and never reaches biometrics. The reply deadline is 7 days from delivery.
- **If the case has not moved at all for a long time,** there is a formal tool - a ponaglenie (complaint about the authority's inaction) under article 37 of the KPA. This is a lawful way to remind the wojewoda of the deadlines, and if silence continues, the path to a skarga before the Wojewódzki Sąd Administracyjny. How this works in practice is covered in our piece on [speeding up the karta pobytu via ponaglenie and the WSA](/en/blog/uskorenie-karty-pobytu-ponaglenie-wsa).

Do not confuse two things: a delayed biometrics and a delayed decyzja are different stages. A long wait for the decision itself (8-14 months in Mazovia) is, unfortunately, the norm, and a ponaglenie does not always work against it. But an abnormally long absence of even the biometrics summons is a reason to check eDoręczenia and, if needed, remind the office of your case.

## Can you reschedule the termin

Yes. An assigned biometrics termin can be moved once for free - through your account or by contacting the office. This is convenient if the date falls on a trip, an illness, or a work conflict.

Further postponements are at the office's discretion, and here it pays to be careful. Repeated rescheduling drags the case out: until biometrics are taken, no decyzja is issued. So keep the first free postponement "in reserve" for a genuinely unforeseen situation, rather than spending it on a minor inconvenience.

## Where to collect the finished card

When the decyzja is positive and the card is produced by the Polska Wytwórnia Papierów Wartościowych, a readiness notice arrives in eDoręczenia. In Mazovia, the finished karta pobytu is issued at ul. Marszałkowska 3/5 - regardless of where you gave your biometrics.

You can collect the card in person during office hours; no separate "booking" is needed. Bring your passport and the readiness notice. From the moment you collect the card your PESEL is active, and in the mObywatel app you can generate an mDowód.

## inPOL vs MOS v2.0: how the "booking" changed

To fit the 2025 reform into one picture, let us compare the two worlds through that very "booking" experience people search this article for.

| Parameter | inPOL (how it was) | MOS v2.0 (how it is now) |
|---|---|---|
| Filing documents | in-person visit at the office counter | online, from home, no visit |
| "Booking" to file | you had to hunt for a queue slot | not needed - file any day |
| In-person visit remains for | submitting all documents + biometrics | biometrics only |
| Who sets the visit date | the applicant, hunting for a slot | the office itself, via eDoręczenia |
| Time to the in-person visit | unpredictable, weeks of hunting | biometrics termin 30-60 days after the UPO |
| Correspondence with the wojewoda | paper letters | eDoręczenia, reply within 7 days |
| Proof of legality | a stamp in the passport | UPO + electronic status |

The main takeaway from the table: the effort shifted. Energy used to go into simply getting into the office. Now getting in is easy - what is hard is filling in the wniosek correctly and not missing the wojewoda's request in eDoręczenia.

## When it is worth involving a lawyer

You can file a first wniosek through MOS v2.0 yourself if the basis is simple (work at a large employer experienced with foreigners), you already have Profil Zaufany and a PESEL, and there are no problems from the past - refusals or returns.
