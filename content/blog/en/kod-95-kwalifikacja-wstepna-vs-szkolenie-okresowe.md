# Code 95 in Poland 2026: Long Course (Kwalifikacja Wstępna) vs Short Course (Szkolenie Okresowe)

Short version: from August 2025, Ukrainian, Belarusian and other foreign international drivers in categories C/CE/D/DE must hold an EU-format Code 95 (świadectwo kwalifikacji zawodowej kierowcy). There are two paths to it, and **the choice depends solely on the issue date of the category C licence** in the country of origin:

- **Category C licence issued before 10 September 2009** → the **short Szkolenie Okresowe** suffices (35 hours, no exam, ~900 PLN).
- **Category C licence issued on or after 10 September 2009** → the **long Kwalifikacja Wstępna** is required (140 hours, with a state exam, ~2,800 PLN).

## What Code 95 Is

Code 95 is a **special annotation on the licence** (or a separate driver qualification card) - the EU code "95" - confirming that the driver completed mandatory training under Directive 2003/59/EC. Without Code 95 the driver **may not perform commercial transport** in the EU on C/CE/D/DE categories.

In Poland Code 95 is mandatory for:
- truck drivers (C / CE) in commercial transport;
- bus drivers (D / DE) in passenger transport.

Not required for:
- B-category drivers;
- private drivers carrying their own goods only;
- agricultural / construction equipment within 50 km of base.

From 2025-2026 **Polish ITD has begun mass roadside Code 95 checks** for all commercial driver categories, including those on umowa zlecenie or B2B. Missing - 8,000 PLN fine on the employer.

## The 10 September 2009 Date - Why

The date when Directive 2003/59/EC entered force in Poland. Drivers holding category C/CE before that have "acquired rights" (prawa nabyte). After - Kwalifikacja Wstępna was mandatory at the time of issuance.

Foreign licences follow the same logic. Issue date before 10 September 2009 - short course. After - long.

**Where the issue date is shown:**
- Russian licences - "дата выдачи".
- Ukrainian - "дата видачі".
- Belarusian - "дата выдачі".
- Turkish - "verili tarih".

If the licence has been exchanged or extended - the original first-issue date for category C/CE is what counts.

## Szkolenie Okresowe (Short Course)

**Who:** drivers with C licences issued before 10.09.2009.

**Structure:**
- 35 hours of theory (typically 5 days × 7 hours);
- Topics: updated traffic rules, eco-driving, ergonomics, cargo safety, new techniques.
- **No state exam.**

**Cost:** 800-1,200 PLN in Mazowieckie 2026, depending on the OSK. LegalWin recommends 4+ vetted schools in Warsaw.

**Timeline:** 5 days of course + 1 week paperwork = 2 weeks max.

**Documents:**
- category C/CE licence (original + sworn translation if foreign);
- karta pobytu or stay confirmation;
- medical from authorised physician;
- transport psychologist test (300-400 PLN);
- 2 photos 3.5 × 4.5 cm.

**On completion:**
- certificate of Szkolenie Okresowe;
- entry in profil kierowcy zawodowego (PKZ) at Wydział Komunikacji;
- driver qualification card (or Code 95 entered on the licence).

Code 95 validity after Szkolenie Okresowe - 5 years. Then - repeat Szkolenie Okresowe (35 hours).

## Kwalifikacja Wstępna (Long Course)

**Who:** drivers with C licences issued on or after 10.09.2009.

**Structure:**
- 140 hours of theory and practice (typically 4-6 weeks, 5 days/week);
- Topics as Szkolenie Okresowe plus deeper: legislation, working hours, emergency preparedness, ADR foundations.
- **State exam at WORD** on completion.

**State exam:**
- Theory + practice (driving).
- 30 theory items (1 point each), pass mark 26 of 30.
- Practice - driving with a WORD examiner on a defined route.
- Exam fee ~200 PLN. Retake ~200 PLN.

**Cost:** 2,500-3,200 PLN in Mazowieckie 2026.

**Timeline:** 4-6 weeks of course + 2-3 weeks for the state exam = 7-9 weeks.

**Documents:** same as Szkolenie Okresowe, plus confirmation that C was issued on or after 10.09.2009 (apostilled certificate from country of issue if disputed).

**On completion:**
- certificate of Kwalifikacja Wstępna (state-level);
- PKZ + driver qualification card.

Code 95 validity after Kwalifikacja Wstępna - 5 years. Then - Szkolenie Okresowe (35 hours) every 5 years.

## Common Mistakes

**1. Assuming the short course is enough.** Licence C issued in 2010 - Szkolenie Okresowe is invalid. ITD on the road checks the date, and with a mismatch - Code 95 is treated as invalid.

**2. Falsifying the date on the licence.** Criminal liability. Polish databases can verify with the country of issue via consulate.

**3. Skipping the psychologist.** Without psych certification, PKZ is not activated. Five days of course wasted.

**4. Non-accredited course.** Only accredited OSKs issue ITD-recognised certificates. List - on the Wojewoda website.

**5. Late renewal.** Code 95 - 5 years. New Szkolenie Okresowe must be done 6 months before expiry. Late - loss of commercial driving rights until completion.

## Hybrid Formats

Since 2024 the Ministry of Infrastructure permits part of the course (up to 50 % of Szkolenie Okresowe and 30 % of Kwalifikacja Wstępna) to run **online** via approved platforms. Convenient for international drivers who can study evenings between trips. Fully online is not allowed - practice and final assessment must be offline.
