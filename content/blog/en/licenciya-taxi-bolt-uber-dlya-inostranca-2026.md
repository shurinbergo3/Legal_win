# Driving for taxis, Bolt and Uber in Poland: licences for foreigners 2026

Poland rebuilt its passenger transport market over the past two years. "Arrive, sign up in the app, start driving" no longer works: requirements tightened on 17 June 2024, and the transitional period for drivers already working ended on 17 June 2025.

In short: **you need a Polish or EU driving licence, a licence to carry passengers, medical and psychological clearance, a criminal record certificate, and in-person identity verification in the app.**

## The headline change: Polish or EU licences only

A driver carrying passengers in a car must hold a driving licence issued **in Poland or another EU or EEA state**. Ukrainian, Belarusian, Georgian, Turkish and other national licences do not qualify for this work, regardless of whether they remain valid for ordinary driving.

That single requirement removed a large share of drivers from the market in 2025.

Exchanging a licence is a separate procedure and, for many countries, includes a theory or practical exam at a WORD test centre. We covered deadlines and documents [in detail](/en/blog/obmen-prav-na-polskie-2026), and the PKK profile and exam [here](/en/blog/obmen-prav-pkk-zapis-na-egzamin-word-2026).

The general rule applies too: living in Poland for more than 185 days a year makes the exchange mandatory regardless of driving work.

## What else the driver needs

| Requirement | Issued by | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Category B licence, Polish or EU | WORD, district office | at least three years' experience |
| Medical examination | occupational health doctor | for professional drivers |
| Psychological examination | transport psychologist | mandatory since 2024 |
| Criminal record certificate | KRK | for a defined range of offences |
| Legal residence with the right to work | wojewoda | residence card, UKR status and others |
| Identity verification | the app operator | in person, with documents |

The vetted offences cover intentional crimes against life and health, sexual freedom and decency, traffic safety, property, economic transactions and the reliability of documents, plus fiscal offences. A foreigner also needs a certificate from their country of citizenship with a sworn translation - [how to obtain one](/en/blog/spravka-o-nesudimosti-iz-strany-proishozhdeniya).

## The licences: which one and for whom

This is where confusion usually starts, because there are two.

**A national road transport licence for carrying passengers by car**, issued by the district authority. It belongs to the entrepreneur - whoever runs the business. Indicative fees: around PLN 700 for 2-15 years, PLN 800 for 15-30 years, PLN 900 for 30-50 years. Each vehicle gets an extract from the licence.

**A taxi licence**, issued by the municipality, covers taxi work with a meter and markings. Cheaper - roughly PLN 200-300 depending on the term.

App-based work usually runs on the first licence. Alongside it you need:

- **the right to use the vehicle** - ownership, leasing or a contract;
- **the entrepreneur's criminal record certificate**;
- **no arrears** at ZUS or the tax office - [how to confirm](/en/blog/zaswiadczenia-zus-us-o-niezaleganiu-2026);
- **carrier liability insurance**.

## Two routes for a foreigner

**Route 1: your own business with a licence.** You register the activity, hold the licence in your own name and work directly with the app operator. More freedom and higher income, but all the administration is yours: social security, tax, reporting, the licence itself.

The constraint: sole trader status is not open to every foreigner - a qualifying residence title is needed. [The available grounds](/en/blog/jdg-dlya-inostrancev-v-polshe).

**Route 2: working for a licensed partner.** A fleet or partner company holds the licence and you work on a civil-law or employment contract. Less administration, less income, but available without the right to run a business.

Verify the partner: a valid licence and a correct contract form. Working for an unlicensed operator is a risk for the driver too, not only the company.

## Liability and penalties

Carrying passengers without a licence attracts an administrative penalty on the entrepreneur, reaching several thousand zloty. The driver is separately liable for missing documents during a road transport inspection or police check.

Checks in the app market have become routine, and operators are required to verify driver documents and block accounts where they do not match.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I drive for Bolt on a Ukrainian licence?**
Not for carrying passengers. A licence issued in Poland or another EU or EEA state is required.

**How long does the exchange take?**
Three weeks to several months, depending on the issuing country and whether an exam applies. Start early rather than in the week you plan to begin work.

**Do I need a licence if I work for a fleet?**
The licence belongs to whoever runs the business. Working under contract with a licensed partner, you do not obtain your own, but the driver requirements still apply to you.

**How long are the medical checks valid?**
Medical and psychological clearances have defined validity periods, and renewing them is the driver's responsibility.

**Can I drive a rented car?**
Yes, with documentation proving your right to use it and subject to the operator's requirements on vehicle age and class.

**Does driving work count as grounds for a residence card?**
Not on its own, but it can support one either as employment with a Polish employer or as running a business. The structure needs planning in advance.
