# Karta CUKR in Poland for Ukrainians - Complete 2026 Guide

The karta CUKR (formal name: "zezwolenie na pobyt czasowy dla obywatela Ukrainy") is a special residence permit available only to Ukrainian citizens who hold an active temporary-protection status (PESEL UKR) at the time of filing. Applications under the current procedure opened on **4 May 2026** and close on **4 March 2027** - a hard window that nobody has promised to extend. Introduced by the "specustawa o pomocy obywatelom Ukrainy" in March 2022 and reworked since, it now grants a three-year residence permit without comparing salary to the minimum wage and without requiring a specific basis such as employment or business.

Below - what distinguishes CUKR from an ordinary karta czasowego pobytu, who it actually suits, how to obtain it in 2026, and what happens after temporary protection expires in March 2027.

## At a Glance: CUKR vs Regular Karta Pobytu

| Parameter | Karta CUKR | Regular karta czasowego |
|---|---|---|
| Eligible to apply | UA citizens with active PESEL UKR | any foreigners |
| Validity | 3 years automatically | up to 3 years, usually 1-2 |
| Minimum salary | not required | 4,806 PLN gross in 2026 |
| Tied to employer | no | yes |
| Counts toward stałe residence | yes (since 2024) | yes |
| Fees | 340 PLN opłata skarbowa + 100 PLN for the card | 340 PLN + 100 PLN |
| Processing time in Mazowieckie | 3-6 months | 8-14 months |

The main advantage of CUKR is the freedom to change employers without notification and faster processing. The main risk is that its existence depends on a political decision to extend EU temporary protection (uncertain after March 2027).

## Who Is Eligible for CUKR

Conditions (Ustawa o pomocy obywatelom Ukrainy, art. 38-1):

1. **Ukrainian citizenship** - confirmed by a Ukrainian passport
2. **Active UKR status** - that is, a PESEL with the UKR marking (issued after 24 February 2022 and not expired)
3. **At least 365 days** of continuous UKR status at the moment of filing
4. **A current basis** for staying in Poland - work, business, study, family, or simply the temporary-protection regime itself

If your PESEL UKR was issued six months ago, wait six more. If a child's UKR was activated at birth in Poland, file as soon as the 365-day threshold is reached.

## Step 1. Document Preparation

The base set is minimal compared with a regular karta pobytu:

- Completed wniosek (via MOS v2.0)
- Scan of the Ukrainian passport (photo page + propiska page)
- Scan confirming PESEL UKR (available from the mObywatel app)
- Two biometric photographs 35 × 45 mm
- Confirmation of opłaty skarbowej payment of 100 PLN
- Proof of accommodation in Poland (lease, meldunek, or umowa użyczenia from resident acquaintances)

For minors - add the birth certificate with sworn translation (about 50 PLN).

No employer Załącznik nr 1, no ZUS confirmations, no PIT-11. CUKR is the simplest in terms of documentation.

## Step 2. Filing via MOS v2.0

Since 27 April 2026, filing is exclusively via mos.cudzoziemcy.gov.pl. Login via your Profil Zaufany (mandatory for CUKR - this is the same system Polish citizens use).

In MOS v2.0:

1. Create a case of type "Wniosek o wyrobienie CUKR"
2. Complete basic data (most autofills from PESEL UKR)
3. Attach 4 files (passport, PESEL, photograph, payment confirmation)
4. Sign with Profil Zaufany
5. Receive UPO with case number

From the UPO, you are exempt from the standard legal-stay requirements of an ordinary karta - you already hold PESEL UKR + UPO.

## Step 3. Biometrics and Waiting

Biometrics for CUKR in Mazowieckie take place at the standard locations: al. Solidarności 81 or ul. Kruczej 5/11. The summons arrives via eDoręczenia, usually 30-45 days after UPO.

Waiting time for the decision - 3-6 months in Mazowieckie in 2026. Substantially faster than a regular karta czasowego, because the voivode processes CUKR in a separate queue with simplified procedure.

## Step 4. Card Collection

After a positive decision (delivered to eDoręczenia):

- 7 days to pay 100 PLN for the physical card
- Card is printed in 7-14 days
- Personal collection in Mazowieckie at ul. Marszałkowska 3/5

The card is valid for 3 years from the issue date. You may work for any employer (no attachment), run a business, study, travel within the Schengen area - all rights are the same as on a regular karta czasowego pobytu, plus the freedom to change jobs without notification.

## What Happens After CUKR Expires

CUKR is for 3 years; afterwards you choose a path:

1. **Apply for karta stałego pobytu** (if 5 years of combined UKR + CUKR are accumulated and you have passed B1)
2. **Apply for a new CUKR** (if temporary protection is still active)
3. **Switch to a regular karta czasowego pobytu** (if you have a basis - work, business, family)

As of 2026 it is unclear what will happen to temporary protection after March 2027. If it is not extended, a subsequent CUKR may not be possible, and you will need to switch to a regular karta.

## Most Common Mistakes When Applying for CUKR

1. **Filing before 365 days of UKR.** The wniosek will be returned as "niespełnienie warunków".
2. **Using an old PESEL (without UKR marking).** The PESEL must be issued with UKR, not "ordinary".
3. **Ignoring eDoręczenia.** The voivode may request uzupełnienia (e.g., a current meldunek).
4. **Filing from the country of citizenship.** CUKR can only be filed from inside Poland.
5. **Duplicating procedures.** If you already hold a regular karta czasowego pobytu, you cannot file CUKR in parallel. Annul the old one first, then file the new one.
