# Lost or Stolen Karta Pobytu: How to Get a Duplicate (Wtórnik) in 2026

Your karta pobytu (residence card) is gone, and the first thing you feel is a small wave of panic. Without it you cannot confirm your identity at the bank, prove that you are in the country legally during a check, or cross the border without extra hassle. The good news: this is a fixable problem with its own dedicated procedure - the issuing of a wtórnik (duplicate). The bad news: you have only 14 days to notify the wojewoda (regional governor), and you need that card in your hand pretty much every day.

Below we walk through what to do in the first hours, how to file for a duplicate, what it costs in 2026, and how to avoid the trap of leaving the country without your card.

---

## What to Do the Moment Your Card Goes Missing

If your karta pobytu is lost or stolen, you have 14 days to report the loss to the wojewoda who issued it. If it was stolen, file a police report first and get the case number - that record protects you if someone else tries to use the card. Then you submit a wniosek (application) for a wtórnik of the same card.

From there, work through it calmly and in order. On this first step, the difference between a loss and a theft really matters.

### If It Was Stolen, Go to the Police First

A stolen karta pobytu is more than a lost piece of plastic. A stranger now holds a document with your personal data, your PESEL number, and your photo. It can be used to take out a loan, register something in your name, or run other scams.

So if the card was stolen, your first stop is the komisariat policji (police station) to file a zawiadomienie o kradzieży (theft report). Ask for a potwierdzenie (confirmation) with the case number. That document earns its keep twice: it clears you of responsibility for whatever a third party does with the card, and it attaches to your wtórnik application as grounds.

### If You Simply Lost It, Log the Loss

If the card just went missing - slipped out, got left in a taxi, disappeared during a move - a police report is not mandatory, though it does no harm. What matters here is notifying the wojewoda in time. The clock on those 14 days starts the moment you discover the loss.

In practice, in Mazowieckie we advise clients not to drag this out to the last day. The sooner you start the procedure, the sooner you have a zaświadczenie (certificate) in hand that confirms your legal stay while the new card is being produced.

---

## The 14-Day Rule: Why You Cannot Stall

The duty to notify the wojewoda of a lost karta pobytu within 14 days is set out in the ustawa o cudzoziemcach (Act on Foreigners). The count runs from the day you learned of the loss, not the day you got around to dealing with it.

What happens if you miss the deadline? In a standard case there is usually no formal fine for being late, but delay hits you in other ways:

- for that whole stretch you have no valid document proving your right to stay;
- any check on the street, at the bank, or with an employer turns into a problem;
- if the card was stolen and someone used it, waiting makes it harder to prove it was not you.

We often see people put off the notification, telling themselves "the card will turn up soon." Sometimes it does. More often a month goes by and the person is still without a document and without a started procedure. Do not repeat that mistake - file the notification right away.

## What a Wtórnik Is and When You Need One

A wtórnik is a duplicate of your karta pobytu, issued to replace one that was lost, stolen, or destroyed. Here is the key point: your status itself - the zezwolenie na pobyt (residence permit) - stays exactly where it was. You do not file a fresh application, you do not re-prove your grounds for staying, and you do not restart the procedure from zero. The permit is still valid; only the physical carrier, the plastic card, is being replaced.

You need a wtórnik in a few situations:

- the karta pobytu is lost;
- the karta pobytu is stolen;
- the card is damaged or destroyed (washed, snapped, chip failure);
- the data printed on the card has changed.

A wtórnik carries the same validity date as your permit. If your karta czasowego pobytu (temporary residence card) was issued through, say, 2027, the duplicate runs to that same date - no new countdown begins.

## How to File for a Wtórnik in 2026

From 2026, applications for residence cards in Mazowieckie are filed electronically through the MOS 2.0 system. That includes the wtórnik application. Here is the procedure step by step.

### Step 1: Gather Your Documents

The set for a duplicate is small:

| Document | Note |
|----------|------|
| Wniosek for a wtórnik | Completed in the system |
| Passport | Original plus a scan of the data page |
| Photo 3.5 x 4.5 cm | Current, white background |
| Potwierdzenie z policji | If the card was stolen |
| Payment confirmation | Scan of the payment receipt |

### Step 2: File Through MOS 2.0

From 2026, wojewodas no longer accept paper applications for karta czasowego pobytu, stałego pobytu (permanent residence), or rezydenta długoterminowego UE (EU long-term resident) - everything goes electronically through MOS 2.0 (mos.cudzoziemcy.gov.pl). To log in you need a Profil Zaufany (trusted profile) or a podpis kwalifikowany (qualified electronic signature). Choose the application type tied to issuing a wtórnik, fill in the details, and attach your scans.

If you do not have a Profil Zaufany yet, sort that out ahead of time - without it you cannot get into the system. We covered how to set one up in our article on [PESEL, Profil Zaufany, and mObywatel](/en/blog/pesel-profil-zaufany-mdowod-posle-karty-pobytu).

### Step 3: Pay and Wait for It to Be Ready

The fee for producing the plastic card is 100 PLN. Payment goes through the system online, and the potwierdzenie attaches to your application. Once the wniosek is accepted you receive a zaświadczenie, and when the duplicate is ready you are called in to collect it.

## How to Prove Legal Stay Without the Card

The big fear in this situation: "I have no document, so I must be illegal." Not true. Losing the plastic does not cancel your residence permit. You confirm your legal stay by other means:

- **Zaświadczenie confirming the wtórnik application** - your main document for the waiting period. Keep it on you.
- **Potwierdzenie z policji** in a theft case - shows the circumstances that left you without the card.
- **Data in the registers** - your status is visible to the wojewoda and the border service in the system, even with no plastic in your hand.

A separate word on mObywatel and mDowód: the digital services are handy for plenty of situations inside Poland, but they do not replace the karta pobytu when you cross the border. More on that in the next section.

## Leaving the Country Without Your Karta Pobytu: The Main Trap

This is where people make the costliest mistake. The logic sounds simple: "the permit is valid, my status is in the system, so I can travel freely." In practice it is far stricter.

The karta pobytu is the document that, together with your passport, gives you the right to enter Poland and move around the Schengen area. Without the physical card you:

- risk not getting back into Poland without a separate visa if you traveled outside Schengen;
- may run into problems during checks inside Schengen;
- are effectively tied to Polish territory until the duplicate arrives.

The zaświadczenie confirming your wtórnik application legalizes your stay inside Poland, but on its own it is not a travel document and does not guarantee a smooth return from abroad. So our advice is blunt: until you have the duplicate in hand, put serious trips outside the country on hold. If travel is unavoidable, get a consultation first, because the route has to be built individually, sometimes through a visa.

## Common Mistakes

Over years of practice we have collected the things people trip over most often:

1. **Stalling on the notification.** "Maybe it will turn up" - and the 14 days quietly run out.
2. **Skipping the police after a theft.** Then a loan taken out on their card surfaces later.
3. **Traveling abroad on a zaświadczenie alone.** And getting stuck, unable to return without a visa.
4. **Confusing a wtórnik with a fresh application.** They start gathering documents on grounds for staying, even though the permit is valid and only a duplicate is needed.
5. **Forgetting to collect the finished card.** From 2026 there is a rule: if you do not pick up your ready karta within 60 days of the urząd's notice, the permit is not issued and the card is annulled. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long do I have to notify the wojewoda about a lost card?**
14 days from the moment you discover the loss. That deadline comes from the ustawa o cudzoziemcach. The count runs from the day of discovery, not the day you decide to deal with it, so file the notification right away.

**How much does a wtórnik karty pobytu cost in 2026?**
The fee to produce the plastic card is 100 PLN. There is no separate large opłata skarbowa (stamp duty) like the one on a first permit application, because the zezwolenie itself is already valid and is not being issued again.

**Do I have to re-prove my grounds for staying when I file for a wtórnik?**
No. A wtórnik replaces only the physical card; your residence permit keeps running. You do not re-gather documents on work, income, and housing - the set for a duplicate is minimal.

**Can I travel abroad while the duplicate is being made?**
Not recommended. The zaświadczenie for a wtórnik application legalizes your stay inside Poland, but it does not guarantee a smooth return from abroad - without the card you risk not getting back in without a separate visa. Hold off on serious trips until you have the plastic.

**What do I do if my card was stolen?**
First file a zawiadomienie o kradzieży with the police and get a potwierdzenie with the case number. Then notify the wojewoda and submit a wtórnik application, attaching the police record. That record protects you if a stranger uses the card.

**Does mObywatel replace a lost karta pobytu?**
Inside Poland the digital services are handy in many situations, but they do not replace the karta pobytu at the border and are not a travel document. For trips you need the physical card.
