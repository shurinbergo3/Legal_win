# Driving Licence Exchange for US, UK and Indian Nationals in Poland: Sitting the WORD Theory Exam

Short version: Ukrainians, Belarusians, Russians, Georgians exchange via an administrative procedure with no exam. **Citizens of the US, post-Brexit UK, India, the UAE, and most provinces of Canada** must sit a **theory exam at WORD** (Wojewódzki Ośrodek Ruchu Drogowego) — available in Polish or English, 50 PLN, 30 questions, pass mark 68 of 74. About 70 % of foreigners pass on first attempt in Mazowieckie. Full procedure 6–10 weeks.

## Who Must Sit WORD

Poland recognises licences from countries party to the **1968 Vienna Convention** in full — most EU, CIS, Turkey, Japan, South Korea — without exam.

For other countries — exam at WORD:

| Country | Reason |
|---|---|
| USA | Not party to Vienna |
| Canada | Party but with limited implementation |
| UK (post-Brexit) | EU-equivalent before; third country after |
| India | Not party to Vienna |
| UAE | Not party |
| Australia | Not party |
| New Zealand | Not party |
| Most of Africa | Not party or with reservations |

Citizens of these countries residing in Poland on **a karta pobytu** for more than 185 days must exchange via the WORD route. Without exchange — the same fines as in [Article 19](/en/blog/pravilo-185-dney-itd-shtrafy).

## Procedure

**Step 1. Sworn translation of the licence.**

English / Indian licence translated into Polish — required for PKK. 80–150 PLN. 3–7 days.

**Step 2. Medical certificate from an authorised physician.**

Standard driving-licence medical. About 200 PLN. Valid 5 years.

**Step 3. Register with a driving school (OSK) to activate PKK.**

Registration with any Ośrodek Szkolenia Kierowców activates the **profil kandydata na kierowcę (PKK)**. No full course needed for foreigners with a valid national licence — exempt from theory and practical training in OSK. Registration takes a day; cost ~100–200 PLN.

In Warsaw — about 5–7 OSKs offering English service. LegalWin recommends specific ones.

**Step 4. Book the exam at WORD.**

WORD in Warsaw — two centres: WORD Warszawa-Bemowo (main) and WORD Warszawa-Targówek. Booking via info-car.pl.

Cost:
- Theory B (passenger cars) — 50 PLN.
- Theory C (trucks) — 50 PLN additional to B.

Exam available **in Polish or English**. English is standard in Mazowieckie due to the international airport flow.

**Step 5. Prepare for the exam.**

Structure:
- 30 questions (20 standard + 10 situational).
- 25 minutes.
- Each question weighs 1, 2 or 3 points (74 max).
- Pass mark — **68 of 74**.

Topics:
- Road signs and signals (~30 %).
- Polish traffic rules — priority, overtaking, parking, speed (40 %).
- Safety — seatbelts, children, alcohol, medication (15 %).
- Vehicle condition (10 %).
- Reaction in atypical situations (5 %).

Preparation: free online textbook at kierowcytestowi.pl (English demos). Paid — 60–120 PLN for 2–4 weeks of access. Realistic preparation for English speakers — 20–30 hours.

**Step 6. Sit the exam.**

Arrive at WORD, register, take the test in a computer room. Result — instant. Pass — receive zaświadczenie. Fail — retake after 7 days (additional 50 PLN).

**Step 7. Wniosek at Wydział Komunikacji.**

With the zaświadczenie from WORD, file at the Wydział Komunikacji of your meldunek. State fee — 100 PLN + 100 PLN card. New licence in 4–6 weeks.

## Common Mistakes

**1. Assuming no exam is needed.** US citizens often rely on the AAA "international driving permit" — a 1-year tourist document. Residents need WORD.

**2. Booking the exam without an active PKK.** WORD will not accept entry without it. Activated only through OSK registration.

**3. Sitting the Polish version without Polish.** English is everywhere in Mazowieckie. Smaller cities (Płock, Radom) may lack English — Warsaw is the safe bet.

**4. Preparing only with UK/US tests.** Polish rules differ — roundabout priority, intersection markings, speed limits in residential zones. Use Polish materials.

**5. Ignoring seasonality.** September–October (post-summer) and April–May — bookings 4–6 weeks out. January–February is open.

## Alternatives for Specific Cases

**Canadian licences.** Certain provinces (Ontario, Quebec, BC) have bilateral agreements — exchange without exam. Verify with the Wydział or a lawyer.

**Pre-Brexit UK licences.** Issued **before 1 January 2021** with Polish residency from before that date — special admin exchange right. Apply within 12 months of relocation.

**Australian licences.** Victoria (VIC) has a bilateral agreement — exchange without exam.
