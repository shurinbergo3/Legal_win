# Speeding Up Karta Pobytu in 2026: Ponaglenie and Court Action Against the Voivode

Short version: by law (Article 35 KPA), the voivode must issue a decision on a karta pobytu application within 1–2 months. In practice in Mazowieckie 2026 — the average is 8–14 months; in Dolnośląskie — 4–6; in Śląskie — up to 36. If your case has been idle for more than 6 months, two legal levers exist: **ponaglenie** (a formal reminder) and **skarga na bezczynność at the Wojewódzki Sąd Administracyjny (WSA)** — a complaint of inaction at the regional administrative court. The court can order a decision and award financial compensation (typically 1,000–5,000 PLN) for the delay.

## Statutory Timelines

Article 35 KPA sets the baseline:

- **30 days** for cases with simple consideration.
- **60 days** for cases needing investigative procedure (most karta pobytu cases).
- Extension possible on substantiated grounds (case complexity), with mandatory notification of the applicant.

In reality these timelines **are not met** in any major voivodeship. Mazowieckie, Wrocław, Śląskie, Pomorskie are all in resource crisis: applications have grown 4–6× since 2022, while staffing only 30 %. Mazowieckie's backlog in 2026 is around 78,000 unresolved cases.

This means **a case can sit perfectly legally for 6+ months** as long as the voivode can invoke "case complexity". After 6 months, however, you have grounds to act.

## Step 1: Ponaglenie

A ponaglenie is a formal reminder to the head of the administrative unit handling the case. For karta pobytu — the **head of the legalisation department at the Voivodeship Office**.

**When to file:**
- More than 60 days from filing (or from the last uzupełnienie).
- More than 6 months from UPO with no activity.
- No requests for supplementary documents, no biometrics summons, no other communication.

**Structure** (Article 37 KPA):

1. Title: "Ponaglenie w sprawie..."
2. Applicant details.
3. MOS v2.0 case number.
4. Chronology: filing date, UPO date, supplementations, current status.
5. Reference to breach of Article 35 KPA.
6. Request:
   - issue the decision within the prescribed period;
   - or notify of extension with a concrete reason;
   - or confirm the breach (which becomes basis for skarga to the WSA).

Filing — through MOS v2.0 (electronic), via ePUAP, or by post.

**Reply:** the voivode must answer within 7 days. Possible outcomes:

- **"Case in progress, please wait" with a concrete timeline** — useful if realistic.
- **"Case completed, decision shortly"** — most common; in 60 % of cases the decision arrives within 30–60 days.
- **"Case ongoing, justified reason"** — a specific reason (e.g., consulate response pending).
- **Ignored** — itself a breach and grounds for direct skarga to the WSA.

## Step 2: Skarga na Bezczynność to WSA

If ponaglenie does not move the case, the next step is a **skarga na bezczynność** at the Wojewódzki Sąd Administracyjny.

**When to file:**
- The reply to the ponaglenie promised a decision but 30–60 days passed without one.
- The voivode ignored the ponaglenie for more than 30 days.
- A self-set deadline was missed.

**Structure:**

1. Addressed to the **Wojewódzki Sąd Administracyjny in Warsaw** (or the regional WSA).
2. Skarżący (applicant) details.
3. Strona przeciwna (Wojewoda Mazowiecki) details.
4. Chronology with attached ponaglenie + reply (or its absence).
5. Wniosek:
   - declare the voivode's inaction;
   - oblige the voivode to rule within a set deadline;
   - award a money sum (compensation) to the applicant.
6. Court fee — 100 PLN.

**Filing deadline:** within 30 days of receiving the reply to the ponaglenie (or the expiry of the 30-day reply period).

**Court process:**
1. Acceptance — 5–10 days.
2. Voivode notified, with 30 days to file an answer.
3. Voivode often issues the decision before the hearing — about 50 % of skarg are resolved this way.
4. If unresolved — hearing scheduled in 4–8 months.
5. Hearing — 1–2 hours.
6. Court decision:
   - declaration of inaction (formal);
   - obligation on voivode to issue within 30 days;
   - financial compensation — 1,000–5,000 PLN typically; up to 15,000 PLN in extreme cases;
   - costs awarded to applicant.

**Effectiveness:** about 78 % of skarg in Mazowieckie 2025–2026 succeed for applicants. Of those, 50 % resolve before the hearing.

## Step 3: After Winning

If the court declares inaction and orders a decision, the voivode has **30 days** to issue. Continued ignoring opens:

- **Skarga z grzywną** — additional complaint for failing to comply, with fines on the specific official (up to 10,000 PLN).
- **Wniosek o egzekucję** through a court bailiff — rarely used here.
- **Skarga odszkodowawcza** — separate claim for damages caused by delay (lost work, impossibility of travel).

## Common Mistakes

**1. Filing ponaglenie too early.** 30 days after filing — no breach yet, ponaglenie rejected. Wait at least 60–90 days.

**2. Internet-template ponaglenie.** Officials have seen thousands. Without case-specific chronology — ignored or formally rejected.

**3. Missing the 30-day skarga deadline.** Exactly 30 days after the ponaglenie reply. Miss it — file a fresh ponaglenie.

**4. No proof of inaction.** The court demands a clear chronology — filing dates, supplementations, replies. Without them — skarga dismissed as premature.

**5. Confusing skarga with appeal.** Skarga accelerates; it does not change a refusal. If the case has substantive issues, the right tool after a refusal is **odwołanie**, not skarga.
