# How Much a Karta Pobytu Costs in Warsaw in 2026: Full Breakdown

When clients ask "how much does a karta pobytu in Warsaw cost," they usually expect a single figure. In reality it is a set of line items, and some of them only surface mid-process. At LegalWin we have handled these cases since 2019, and we see where people underestimate the budget and where they overpay. Below is an honest breakdown, line by line, with no rounding in our favour.

In short: for the "work" basis, doing it yourself, hard costs come to roughly 600-900 PLN (opłata skarbowa, card, photos, translations), plus medical insurance if you are not yet in ZUS. With a lawyer, full support adds from 2,500 PLN on top. That is the realistic range for Mazowieckie in 2026. Now, line by line.

## Mandatory payments

Everyone pays these, regardless of basis and regardless of whether they file alone or with a lawyer. Filing through MOS v2.0 (the electronic application system, in force since December 2025) is free - the online form costs nothing. The payments are separate.

| Item | Amount | When paid |
|---|---|---|
| Opłata skarbowa (stamp duty for processing the wniosek) | 340 PLN | at filing, the receipt is attached to the application |
| Plastik karty (production of the card) | 100 PLN | after a positive decyzja (decision) |
| Biometric photos 35×45 mm | 30-50 PLN | when preparing documents |
| Tłumaczenia przysięgłe (sworn translations) | 200-400 PLN | when preparing documents |
| **DIY minimum** | **670-890 PLN** | - |

The opłata skarbowa of 340 PLN is the fee for the wojewoda reviewing your case, not for the outcome. If you receive an odmowa (refusal), this money is not refunded. The plastik of 100 PLN is paid only after the decyzja is positive: while there is no decision, you do not pay it.

Photos must be biometric, 35×45 mm. Tell the photographer directly "zdjęcie na kartę pobytu" - an ordinary portrait will not meet the requirements for background and head position. In Warsaw they cost 30-50 PLN per set.

Note: filing through MOS v2.0 indeed carries no separate charge for the online form itself, but the opłata skarbowa still has to be paid as a separate transfer to the Warsaw urząd account, with proof attached to the wniosek. The most common mistake at this step is to pay the stamp duty but forget to attach the receipt: the payment formally exists, but it is not visible in the file, so the wojewoda sends a uzupełnienie. That costs no money, but it adds weeks to the timeline.

## Translations and documents

Tłumaczenia przysięgłe - sworn translations - are needed for every document not issued in Polish. That means the passport, diploma, birth or marriage certificates, and other certificates. A tłumacz przysięgły (sworn translator) applies a seal, without which the wojewoda will not accept the document.

The total depends on the number of documents and usually fits within 200-400 PLN. If you only have a passport and one certificate, it will be closer to the lower end. With a passport, diploma and two certificates, closer to the upper end.

A separate item is the apostille. It is needed when foreign civil-status documents - a birth or marriage certificate - require legalization for use in Poland. The apostille is obtained in the country where the document was issued, costs around 60 PLN and, more important than the money, takes time. If your documents already carry an apostille, this item disappears. If not, budget for it in both money and schedule.

A note from practice: do not send for translation what you do not have to translate. Clients sometimes translate the whole passport when the data page and the page showing the current legal basis are enough. That is an unnecessary 100-150 PLN.

## Insurance and the financial basis

Polisa medyczna (medical insurance) with minimum cover of 30,000 € is needed by those not yet covered by ZUS/NFZ through work. If you are formally employed and contributions are paid for you, separate insurance is not needed - ZUS covers healthcare. If you have no ZUS yet, a private polisa runs 80-150 PLN per month.

Here it is important not to confuse two things. The minimum salary for the "work for a Polish employer" basis in 2026 is 4,806 PLN gross (≈3,606 PLN net). This is not a payment or an expense - it is the income threshold you prove with documents. The wojewoda looks at your contract and certificates to confirm you earn no less than the minimum. You do not hand over money, you demonstrate it. But if your contractual salary is below 4,806 PLN gross, it has to be raised through an aneks (an addendum) from the employer, otherwise the "work" basis ends in refusal.

So the financial basis affects the budget indirectly: it is free in itself, but if the contract needs reworking, additional formalities appear, and sometimes additional costs.

One more nuance about insurance: the polisa is needed for the whole period before ZUS cover kicks in. If you are only just starting a job and contributions are not yet being paid for you, while a decyzja in Mazowieckie takes 8-14 months, you will have to keep private insurance for a large part of that time. Spread across months, 80-150 PLN turns into a sum comparable to the mandatory payments. That is why in a realistic budget insurance is counted not as a one-off but monthly - and it is precisely the item most often forgotten when people add up "how much the card costs."

## Lawyer support: how much and when it is worth it

Full legal support, turnkey, at LegalWin costs from 2,500 PLN. That covers diagnosing the basis, gathering and checking the document set, filling in the wniosek correctly in MOS v2.0, monitoring correspondence with the wojewoda through eDoręczenia, and responding to uzupełnienie braków (a request for missing documents).

Whether it is worth it is a question not of status but of the situation.

Filing yourself makes sense if the basis is simple: work for a large employer experienced with foreigners, you already have a Profil Zaufany, no past refusals, and you are ready to spend 20-30 hours reading the Ustawa o cudzoziemcach (the Act on Foreigners) and the MOS v2.0 instructions.

Support is worth it if: the basis is family or business, there was a past refusal or a returned wniosek, the employer has not hired foreigners before, there are complications (an expired visa, leaving during processing, changing employer), or you simply want a predictable outcome. The arithmetic is simple: one mistake on the first filing means not only frustration but months added to the timeline, and on refusal another 340 PLN to file again. Against 8-14 months of waiting in Mazowieckie, the cost of a mistake is higher than the cost of support.

## Hidden costs people forget

These items are invisible at the start but surface for a noticeable share of applicants. They are exactly why a "cheap" DIY filing can end up more expensive than support.

**Uzupełnienie braków.** The wojewoda may request missing documents: a fresh ZUS certificate, an updated contract, an additional translation. Sometimes that means another trip to the translator and another 100-200 PLN. The deadline to respond is usually 7 days, and if you miss it, the case can be left bez rozpoznania (without examination).

**Re-filing after an odmowa.** If a refusal arrives and you decide to file again, that is a new wniosek and a new opłata skarbowa of 340 PLN. The old stamp duty is not refunded. So a refusal doubles the base payment.

**Odwołanie (appeal).** The alternative to re-filing is to challenge the decyzja odmowna within 14 days. That is separate work: analysing exactly what the wojewoda found insufficient and arguing it with reference to specific provisions. It is almost never won on a do-it-yourself basis.

**Apostille after the fact.** If it turns out a foreign certificate had to be legalized and you did not do it, the apostille has to be ordered in the country of issue. That is another 60 PLN or so plus weeks of waiting, sometimes with shipping.

**Notarial pełnomocnictwo.** If you handle the card through a lawyer remotely and want them to act on your behalf, a notarial pełnomocnictwo (power of attorney) may be needed. That is a separate visit to a notary and a small notarial fee.

Here is everything in one range. The figures are for the "work" basis in Mazowieckie in 2026.

| Scenario | What is included | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Bare minimum DIY | opłata skarbowa 340 + card 100 + photos 30-50 + minimal translations | from 600 PLN |
| Realistic DIY | the above + translations at the upper end + insurance + one uzupełnienie | 900-1,400 PLN + insurance 80-150/mo |
| Turnkey with a lawyer | all mandatory payments + LegalWin support | base costs + from 2,500 PLN |

"Bare minimum DIY" is the ideal scenario: documents in order, few translations, no insurance needed, no refusal. "Realistic DIY" reflects what most people end up with anyway: more translations, a private polisa, one request for documents. "Turnkey" adds support but removes the risk of re-filing and the costs that come with it.

This table has to be read correctly. The figures in the DIY columns are not what the card will cost for certain, but the floor in a lucky scenario. One refusal, and the realistic scenario pulls up toward the cost of support, while on the timeline it overshoots it by months in the wrong direction. So the honest comparison is not "600 PLN versus 2,500" but "600 PLN with the risk of refusal versus a predictable turnkey sum."
