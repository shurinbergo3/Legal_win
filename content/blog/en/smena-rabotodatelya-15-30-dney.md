# Changing Employer with Karta Pobytu in 2026: The 15/30-Day Rule and Zmiana Decyzji

Short version: a karta czasowego pobytu on the work basis is tightly tied to a **specific employer, role and salary** stated in the voivode's decision. On dismissal, the foreigner has only **15 days** for written notification to the voivode and **30 days total** to find a new employer — otherwise the karta is automatically annulled. If a new role is found, two different procedures apply: **zmiana decyzji** (faster) or **a fresh wniosek** (for substantive changes). Most foreigners in this situation make one of four typical mistakes and lose status.

## Who Falls Under the 15/30 Rule

Foreigners with **karta czasowego pobytu** on the bases of:

- praca u polskiego pracodawcy;
- delegowanie do pracy w RP;
- praca w zawodzie regulowanym;
- praca sezonowa.

**Does not apply** to:

- karta CUKR (Ukrainians) — no employer tie;
- EU Blue Card — simplified notification;
- karta stałego pobytu / EU resident — no tie;
- studencka karta — different rules;
- karta with "dostęp do rynku pracy" annotation (spouses of stałego, Blue Card, CUKR holders) — unrestricted.

If you hold an **ordinary work karta** — the 15/30 rule applies.

## Post-Dismissal Timeline

**Day 0.** Notice received or porozumienie signed. The clock starts.

**Days 0–7.** Document preparation:
- copy of wypowiedzenie or porozumienie;
- fresh świadectwo pracy;
- PIT-11 / RMUA from the employer;
- if a job offer is in hand — copy of the future contract.

**By day 15.** **Written notification to the voivode** through MOS v2.0. Without it — even if a new job is found in 5 days — the karta is at risk of annulment.

The notification states:
- end date with the previous employer;
- whether a new employer is in place;
- new contract details if applicable.

**Days 15–45 (or by day 30 from dismissal).** Job search. Most voivodeships count "30 days" from notification, not dismissal — but Mazowieckie occasionally counts from dismissal. Verify.

**After hiring.** File zmiana decyzji or a fresh wniosek (see below).

## Zmiana Decyzji vs Fresh Wniosek

### When zmiana decyzji applies

Changes within the same voivodeship and same legal basis:

- role or salary change with the same employer — zmiana decyzji.
- new employer in Mazowieckie, same contract type and similar role — zmiana decyzji.
- salary up (or unchanged) — zmiana decyzji.

**Documents:**
- Wniosek o zmianę decyzji.
- Załącznik nr 1 from the new employer.
- New contract.
- Confirmation of end of previous contract.
- 50 PLN state fee (vs 340 for a new wniosek).

**Processing:** 1–3 months in Mazowieckie 2026.

**Can you work in the meantime?** Yes if:
- the new employer has an oświadczenie or zezwolenie typu A on you;
- or — if you are a citizen of the five countries (UA/BY/MD/GE/AM), an oświadczenie registered at PUP allows immediate start.

### When a fresh wniosek is required

For substantive changes:

- relocation to a different voivodeship;
- change of legal basis (work → business; work → study);
- different regulated profession;
- karta about to expire (under 90 days remaining).

**Documents:** full first-time pack. Fee 340 PLN.

**Processing:** 6–10 months in Mazowieckie.

**Can you work?** Only with UPO from MOS v2.0 plus oświadczenie/zezwolenie at the new employer.

## The Article 88g Trap

If, in the 30 days between dismissal and the new oświadczenie/zezwolenie registration, you "work" — even informally, even a test day — that is **illegal employment** under Article 88g. Consequences:

- the employer is not obligated to pay for that period;
- 3,000 PLN fine on the employer;
- risk of refusal on the new permit.

Either there is a permit, or you are at home.

## Dismissal With a Dispute

Special case: dismissed unfairly — no cause, late wages, contested wypowiedzenie. Two parallel matters:

1. **Immigration** — 15/30 days running, need a new role.
2. **Employment** — claim at the labour court for unfair termination or unpaid wages.

Mistake: handling only one. Right approach: parallel — filter new employers while filing a wrongful-termination claim (21-day window from receiving wypowiedzenie).

## Common Mistakes

**1. Not notifying the voivode.** The foreigner is dismissed, finds a new role in 7 days, starts work, and assumes all is fine. Six months later — annulment for non-notification.

**2. Filing a fresh wniosek when zmiana decyzji suffices.** 340 PLN instead of 50, plus 6 months instead of 3.

**3. Filing zmiana decyzji on inter-voivodeship moves.** The Mazowieckie voivode lacks competence over another region's decision. The wniosek is returned, time lost.

**4. Working between jobs without oświadczenie.** Test work, internships, "trying it out" — all illegal without separate registration.

**5. Ignoring the 90-day card limit.** If the karta expires in 80 days and you are mid-process — it lapses before the new decision, and you lose status.

## When to Engage a Lawyer

Self-handling works if: same voivodeship, same role, oświadczenie/zezwolenie ready, karta valid 6+ months.

Engage a lawyer if:

- inter-voivodeship relocation;
- change of legal basis;
- karta expires within 4 months;
- previous declaration was incorrect;
- parallel labour dispute;
- Blue Card eligibility (if salary suffices — Blue Card removes the 15/30 rule going forward).
